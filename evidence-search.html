Evidence-Search

Evidence-based clinical Q&A for medical staff. Retrieves verified literature across five databases, surfaces evidence quality, and returns citable answers — not prior knowledge.

Retrieval pipeline
S0
Question breakdown waits for confirmation
Normalizes drugs & lab values, parses PICO, decomposes into prioritized sub-questions, surfaces clinical signals and ambiguities with severity tags.
S1
PubMed MCP search automatic
Builds Boolean MeSH queries per sub-question. Retrieves PMIDs + abstracts. Assigns unique ref_ids — the only identifiers permitted in citations.
S2
Coverage assessment user decides
Prints per-sub-question coverage (Sufficient / Partial / Insufficient). Offers source expansion, or auto-proceeds if a bypass mode is set.
S3
Secondary retrieval optional
Europe PMC full-text, Consensus semantic search, ClinicalTrials active trials, OpenAlex citation graph. Deduplication by PMID > DOI > NCT > title.
∑
Synthesis
Citation-enforced answer in two layers: bottom-line + evidence grade (Layer 1), then full per-sub-question breakdown on request (Layer 2). Second-pass citation verification on all output.
Highlighted feature — inconclusive evidence handling
Non-directive mode
When evidence is insufficient, the skill presents rather than concludes
If the retrieved corpus yields Low or Very Low quality evidence — or if core sub-questions surface conflicting results — the skill does not issue a recommendation. Instead, it lays out the available evidence transparently so the clinician can make an informed judgment. Conflicts between studies are named explicitly ("S3 reports X; S7 reports Y") rather than resolved by the model.
Sufficient evidence
Confident bottom-line answer with overall evidence grade, followed by per-sub-question breakdown and reference list on request.
Weak or conflicting evidence
Evidence presented without a verdict. Studies surfaced with their findings, populations, and limitations. Clinician retains full decision authority.
Key capabilities
Anti-hallucination by design
Only claims backed by corpus refs appear in the answer. No prior knowledge, no fabricated citations.
Evidence quality rating
Every answer carries an overall evidence grade. Conflicts in corpus are surfaced explicitly, not silently resolved.
PICO decomposition
Complex questions are split into prioritized sub-questions — Core, Supporting, Optional — so coverage is granular.
Bypass modes
"urgent" skips expansion for speed. "deep" auto-enables all secondary sources. Both still print the coverage summary.
Drug & lab normalization
Brand names mapped to generics. Lab values interpreted with standard ranges before queries are built.
Verifiable citations
Every reference carries its PMID, DOI, or NCT ID. Links are real and traceable — not generated placeholders.
